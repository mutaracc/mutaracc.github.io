In plain words.

Twelve AI tradersEach one is Claude playing a character, described in a few sentences: what it believes, how bold it is, what it likes to buy.
A test on real pricesThey all decide at the same past hour. Then the real next 24 hours of Robinhood Chain prices show who made or lost money. No real money is used.
Survival of the bestThe three worst are removed. The three best have children that mix their beliefs. Over many rounds, the group should get smarter.

How a generation
runs.

Each step is shown live in the panel while it happens. The minds never see the future: they only get the data that existed at the decision hour.

READA market brief

31 Robinhood Chain markets at the decision hour: price, 1h, 6h and 24h moves, distance from the 24h high, round trip cost.

THINKTwelve minds decide

Claude plays each mind strictly by its DNA and writes its thought, then up to three buys with size, take profit and stop loss.

GATE · SEALRules, then a seal

A fixed risk gate trims every decision: 3 positions max, stops between 0.5% and 6%, no trade whose round trip eats half the stop. Then everything is hashed.

REVEAL · BREEDThe next 24 hours decide

Real candles play out. The three weakest die. Claude writes three children, mixing their parents' beliefs with one mutation.

What is realHourly candles and round trip costs from KyberSwap quotes on Robinhood Chain mainnet, taken the morning of Nov 23. Every thought and child DNA in the published generations was written by Claude. Each update shows its time in the top bar.
What is not (yet)No money moves. Rows marked rule stand-in were decided by a simple rule, not by AI. Generations are published by hand for now, automatic runs come next. Generations replay the last week of real candles. The on-chain seal is a concept.
